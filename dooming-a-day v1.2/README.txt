Dooming a Day - Browser Extension v-1.2
=========================================

Tracked Platforms:
1. YouTube Shorts (https://www.youtube.com/shorts/{id})
2. Instagram Reels (https://www.instagram.com/reel/{id}/ and /reels/{id}/)

INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Unzip this folder to your computer.
2. Open Google Chrome and go to: chrome://extensions
3. Enable "Developer mode" toggle in the top-right corner.
4. Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top-left.
5. Select this unzipped folder.
6. Open YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels and watch your doom counter track each short/reel!
